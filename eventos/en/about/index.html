Pol Delclòs

Event photography and film

I’m Pol Delclòs, a photographer and videographer specialising in event coverage.

I like to tell what happens through images, from the most important moments to the small details that often go unnoticed but are just as much a part of every event.

Over time I’ve learned that good coverage is not only about having a good camera or being there when something important happens. For me it also means adapting to each setting, understanding how the event unfolds, anticipating the key moments and working discreetly, without getting in the way of what is going on.

I like to look after the whole process, from preparation and coverage through to selecting, editing and delivering the material. I spend time going over every photograph, because I believe good coverage should not only look good: it should reflect what happened and add value to the client’s communication.

I work at corporate events, congresses, conferences, product launches and professional gatherings, both for one-off coverage and for ongoing collaborations with companies, agencies and organisers.

My aim is simple: natural, careful and professional coverage that reflects how each event really was and, above all, images that are genuinely useful to the client.
