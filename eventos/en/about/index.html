Pol Delclòs

Event photography and film

I am Pol Delclòs. I photograph and film events. An event happens only once: my job is to come back with the images that tell how it really went — the people, the mood of the room and the moments that do not repeat.

I look after the details, because they are what separate a good set of pictures from a pile of them: reading the light in each room before anyone walks in, being in the right place just before something happens, staying out of the way, and checking every photograph one by one before it is delivered. I work on single events and on a regular basis.
