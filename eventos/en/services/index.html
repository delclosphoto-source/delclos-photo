Services

Every event has something to tell. My job is to turn it into images that not only reflect what happened, but also add value for the people who organise it.

  • 01. Event photography

    Photographic coverage of congresses, conferences, trade fairs, product launches, galas, celebrations and corporate events. From the main moments to the atmosphere, the guests and the small details.

  • 02. Event video

    Filming and editing of videos that gather the highlights of each event. From summary films to short pieces made for websites, social media and communication.

  • 03. Galas, celebrations and shows

    Photography and video of awards ceremonies, company dinners, concerts, shows and celebrations. Coverage that takes in the main moments as well as the atmosphere and the people who are part of the event.

  • 04. Trade fairs, congresses and brand events

    Coverage of professional trade fairs, congresses, brand activations and product launches. Images that document the event and let companies and organisers communicate what they do.
