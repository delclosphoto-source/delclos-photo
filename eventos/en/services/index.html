Services

At an event there is no second chance: either you are there when it happens or you are not. So I arrive early, read the light in each room and decide where I need to stand. I work on single events and on a regular basis.

  • Event coverage

    The whole day or the hours you need: the arrival, what happens on stage, the conversations in the corridors and the close. An ordered account of what took place.

  • Portraits and team

    Portraits of the main faces, the guests and the team, made quickly but without looking rushed.

  • Venue, set-up and details

    The place before anyone walks in: the set-up, the table laid, the signage and the details you took care over that never show up anywhere afterwards.

  • Event film

    A short cut of the day, with the sound and the pace of what happened.
