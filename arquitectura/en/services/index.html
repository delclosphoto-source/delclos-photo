Services

The detail is what separates a correct photograph from a good one: the framing, the hour each shot is taken and what is left out of it. I work on single commissions and on a regular basis, with steady deliveries.

  • Architecture and workplace photography

    Buildings, offices and shared areas: the exterior, the entrances and the floors, with straight verticals and the light each space deserves.

  • Interior and hotel photography

    The materials, the furniture and the details — a joint, a trim, the way a curtain falls — that explain the project, with the mood the space has at each hour of the day.

  • Real estate photography for sales and lettings

    A clear set of images of every home, made for the portals and for sales dossiers, showing the home as it is.

  • Films of spaces

    Short walkthroughs that show how one space leads into the next, and how the light changes along the way.
