Services

A well-photographed space communicates the design, the identity and the value of a project. I work so that companies and practices can show theirs: one-off commissions and ongoing work, with regular deliveries.

  • Architecture and corporate space photography

    Office buildings, receptions, lobbies and shared areas: the exterior, the approaches and the floors, with true verticals and the light each space deserves.

  • Interior and workplace photography

    Offices, meeting rooms, coworking spaces and auditoriums, and hotel interiors too. The materials, the furniture and the details — a joint, a trim, the way a curtain falls — that explain the project.

  • Video of spaces

    Short walkthroughs that show how one space leads into the next and how the light changes along the way.

  • Real-estate photography for sales and lettings

    A clear set of images of each property, made for the portals and the sales packs, showing the space as it is.
