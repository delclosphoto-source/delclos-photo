Pol Delclòs

Architecture, interior and real estate photography and film

I am Pol Delclòs. I photograph and film architecture, interiors and workplaces. My job is to make a project understood in a single image: the proportions of the space, the light it gets at each hour and the materials exactly as they are.

I look after the details, because they are what set a good project apart: straight verticals, faithful colour, a clean frame and every photograph checked one by one before it is delivered. I work with architecture studios, interior designers, developers and estate agencies, on single commissions and on a regular basis.
